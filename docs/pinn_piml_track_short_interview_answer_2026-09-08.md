# Short Interview Answer: PINN/PIML Track

> Yes, I have worked on physics-informed machine learning. Alongside my multimodal solar-flare forecasting project, I created a separate PINN/PIML track. I first used a simple PDE example to demonstrate physics-residual learning, then adapted the principle to solar-flare forecasting using SHARP magnetic proxy variables and auxiliary physical-proxy losses. I present it carefully as physics-informed representation learning, not a full MHD simulator.
