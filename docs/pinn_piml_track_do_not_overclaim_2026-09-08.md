# Do Not Overclaim: PINN/PIML Track

The separate PINN/PIML track is valuable because it is honest and bounded.

## Do not say

- Full MHD PINN for solar flare prediction.
- Physics proves the flare cause.
- The model solves magnetic reconnection.
- PINN guarantees physical correctness.

## Say instead

- Physics-informed machine learning prototype.
- Auxiliary physical-proxy loss.
- Magnetic-energy and helicity-aware representation learning.
- Temporal-consistency regularisation.
- Domain-aware forecasting model.

## Why this matters

Strong interview answers are not built by exaggeration. They are built by showing that the implementation is technically sound and that the boundaries of the claim are understood.
