# Long Interview Answer: PINN/PIML Track

> I have a separate physics-informed machine learning track connected to my space-weather research. I started with a small PINN foundation task using a simple PDE, so I could demonstrate the mechanics of combining data loss with physics-residual loss through automatic differentiation. After that, I designed a solar-flare-relevant physics-informed representation-learning prototype. Instead of claiming a full MHD PINN, I used SHARP magnetic descriptors as physical proxies and added auxiliary objectives to encourage the model representation to preserve information related to flux, free-energy proxies, helicity/current structure, and temporal consistency. This allowed me to show practical physics-informed modelling while keeping the scientific claim honest and bounded.
